## Cross-job repeatability

Preliminary GPU job `189635` executed the same 1,216 prompt tasks using the same model, prompt,
schema, seed, batch size and temperature, but its source commit still enforced a zero-tolerance
article gate. It therefore failed after inference because one preselected scale article produced no
task. Because scale80 is the permitted model-development split, the corpus was kept fixed and the
policy was changed transparently to retain at most two zero-task articles; no reserve was
substituted after output was visible.

A source-free private comparison of the preliminary and accepted task metadata found:

- exact task-set match: 1,216/1,216, with zero missing or extra tasks;
- response SHA-256 match: 1,190/1,216 (97.86%);
- classification match: 1,213/1,216 (99.75%);
- event-count match: 1,212/1,216 (99.67%);
- total accepted events: 99 then 98.

Thus the within-run repeated task was identical, but temperature-zero execution was not bitwise
identical across complete vLLM jobs. The accepted predictions are content-addressed and frozen;
reruns must never silently replace them. This limitation is reported rather than converted into an
accuracy or determinism claim.
