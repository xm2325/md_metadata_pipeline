## Calibration correction

An earlier policy candidate treated multiple engine/force-field values at article scope as a
critical contradiction and routed 38/80 records to double review. That assumption was rejected:
one paper can legitimately compare multiple systems or protocols. The accepted policy makes no
field globally single-valued. Operators may explicitly declare a field single-valued only when a
specific release data product guarantees that cardinality; incompatible values then become a
critical double-review trigger. Model evidence rejection was also narrowed to accepted events that
lost attributes and zero-event records with fully rejected paragraphs. Other rejected paragraphs
are monitored through deterministic QC sampling.

See [`../../docs/HUMAN_REVIEW_POLICY.md`](../../docs/HUMAN_REVIEW_POLICY.md) for the policy and the
conditions that justify forced double review.
